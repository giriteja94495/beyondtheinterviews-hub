FAQ

Do affiliate links cost me anything?

No. Prices are identical whether you use our links or go direct. If you buy through a link, we may earn a small commission that keeps this site running and independent.

Where should I start if I have an interview in 2 weeks?

Coding practice + one mock per week. Start with a free tier (LeetCode, Tech Interview Handbook), then add mocks on Pramp. Skip broad courses when time is short.

How often is this list updated?

Quarterly review minimum, plus updates whenever a product changes pricing or quality drops. Dead weight gets removed, not tolerated.

Do you accept paid placements?

No sponsored slots inside the curated list. If a placement ever exists, it will be labeled explicitly and kept out of rankings.
